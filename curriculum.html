Core Program Syllabus

Learn Stock Market Trading With HEDZO

6 structured modules taking you from stock market basics to full institutional-grade Smart Money & Order Flow analysis.

Structured Learning Path

Explore the 6 Core Modules

Select any module below to view the detailed syllabus breakdown, lesson topics, and practical outcomes.

Module 1 — 6 Lessons

Trading Fundamentals & Market Basics

Build a rock-solid foundation in how financial markets operate, understanding market participants, order types, exchanges, and core market mechanics.

  • What is Stock Market Trading and how it works
  • Primary vs Secondary Markets & Exchanges
  • Order Types: Market, Limit, Stop-Loss & Target Orders
  • Market Participants: Retail vs Institutional Traders
  • Understanding Candlesticks, Timeframes & Volatility
  • Core Trading Terminology & Market Mindset Shift
Module 2 — 7 Lessons

Technical Analysis & Chart Reading

Master the art of chart reading using price trends, support & resistance levels, candlestick formations, and key technical indicators.

  • Support & Resistance identification principles
  • Trendlines, Channels & Chart Patterns
  • Multi-timeframe trend identification (HTF vs LTF)
  • Key Technical Indicators (Moving Averages, RSI, VWAP)
  • Volume Analysis & Price Confluence
  • Charting tools setup & Daily Prep Rituals
Module 3 — 8 Lessons

Price Action & Market Structure

Learn to read structural shifts and price movement without relying on lagging indicators. Understand breakout validity and market momentum.

  • Break of Structure (BOS) & Change of Character (CHoCH)
  • Swing Highs & Swing Lows structural mapping
  • Premium vs Discount Pricing Zones
  • Real Breakouts vs False Breakout Traps
  • Reversal Patterns & Momentum Exhaustion
  • Multi-timeframe price action confluence
Module 4 — 7 Lessons

Smart Money Concepts & Order Flow

Decode how institutional market makers move price. Identify order flow, liquidity pools, fair value gaps, and institutional footprints.

  • Institutional Order Flow & Liquidity Engineering
  • Order Blocks (OB) — Identification, Validation & Mitigation
  • Fair Value Gaps (FVG) / Imbalance Zones
  • Buy-Side & Sell-Side Liquidity Sweeps
  • Liquidity Traps & Institutional Stop Hunts
  • Smart Money entry refinement techniques
Module 5 — 5 Lessons

Risk Management & Capital Protection

Protect your trading capital with strict position sizing rules, risk-to-reward ratios, and structured drawdown management protocols.

  • Position Sizing Formulas & The 1-2% Risk Rule
  • Risk-to-Reward Ratio (RRR) Optimization
  • Stop-Loss Placement & Trailing Stop Strategies
  • Daily & Weekly Drawdown Limits
  • Capital Preservation & Account Scaling Rules
  • Trade Risk Checklist before execution
Module 6 — 6 Lessons

Trading Psychology & Execution Process

Build emotional control, eliminate greed and FOMO, keep a systematic trading journal, and execute your personal trading plan with confidence.

  • Managing FOMO, Revenge Trading & Overtrading
  • Developing a Rule-Based Trading Checklist
  • Trading Journaling & Performance Metrics Tracking
  • Post-Market Debrief & Trade Analytics
  • 3-Month Roadmap Plan (RMP) for Traders
  • Prop Firm Challenge & Live Market Execution Setup
